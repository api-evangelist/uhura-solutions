Efficient document management is essential for staying competitive, ensuring compliance, and maintaining seamless operations. Yet, many companies still rely on manual document processing, unaware of the hidden costs beneath the surface. While traditional filing cabinets and paper forms may seem like low-cost solutions, they can consume more time, money, and resources than most businesses realize.
